# Roblox retention and audience, 2026

Sources: GameAnalytics 2026 Benchmark Report (500+ titles at 1M+ MAU), Roblox newsroom statements 2026. Checked 2026-11-14.

## Medians across the platform

- D1 10.3%
- D7 1.6%
- D30 0.5%

Read that correctly: 0.5% D30 is *normal*, not failure. Most games die. Benchmark against your genre, not against your feelings.

## Roleplay genre

- D30 average ~9%, top titles 15%+
- Average session ~48 minutes, roughly 2x the platform average
- Why: the social graph is the content. Players do not return for systems, they return for people.

## Discovery

- Top 10 games now ~20% of platform hours, down from ~30% three years ago. Hours outside the top 10 grew 25% YoY.
- US 18+ daily users +32%; 18-34 +42%. Japan +67%, India +64%.
- Global Chat averaged 30M messages/day in June 2026, and Roblox is rebuilding chat after age checks cut into it.
- Discovery now weighs a 28-day retention view (D1, D2-7, D8-28) instead of rewarding clickbait thumbnails.

## Money

- Median player opens Roblox ~3x a day and spreads time across several games.
- Spending is concentrated: top 1% of payers spend about $86.36. Per-player monetization at the median is thin.
- Design consequence: build for return frequency and social pull, not for whales.
