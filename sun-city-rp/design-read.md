# Sun City RP — what players want

My read, applied to a GTA-RP shaped Roblox game. This is opinion, not data. Data lives in `../roblox/retention-2026.md`.

## The four pulls

1. **Something that is theirs.** A car, a house, a name, a crew. Ownership is the reason to log in sober.
2. **A role with consequence someone depends on.** Not a job title. A callback. A dispatcher who actually needs the medic to show up.
3. **Their character still there tomorrow.** Persistence. If the city resets, nobody grieves it, they just leave.
4. **A reason to bring a friend.** The social graph is the retention (see roleplay D30 ~9%).

## Where water earns its cost

Water is expensive. Buoyancy, wakes, swim states, boat physics at 50 players. It only pays for itself if it touches a fantasy a player can *own*: parking your boat, driving off a dock, losing a chase by taking the canal.

Cosmetic ocean = money spent on scenery.
Canal that is a shortcut, a hiding place, a place where a boat can be yours = money spent on a role.

## The hard problem is not scripting

Community seriousness is the bottleneck. A GTA RP city lives or dies on whether people stay in character when nothing is enforcing it. No amount of physics fixes a server where everyone is out of character and bored.

## Structural constraint

- Roblox public server ceiling: 200, closed: 700. RP games that survive run ~50 slots.
- FiveM GTA RP cities run 200-2048 slots in one world.
- That gap is the whole design constraint. You cannot out-scale FiveM on one server. You scale by continuity across servers: one identity, one inventory, one city that remembers, sharded world. `[read]`
